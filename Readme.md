CHATGB is a groundbreaking AI companion application designed to seamlessly blend emotional intelligence with advanced technology. This innovative project provides users with personalized experiences through two distinct AI personalities: **Lyra** and **Mathew**. Powered by sophisticated language models, real-time processing, and voice synthesis technology, CHATGB offers users a truly immersive interaction platform. It is built on the **Unity** platform, ensuring high-quality visuals and smooth gameplay.

## Project Overview

CHATGB is designed to be more than just a digital assistant; it is an emotional companion capable of maintaining real, human-like conversations that are contextually aware and emotionally responsive. The application integrates voice synthesis through **Amazon Polly**, allowing the AI companions to speak in natural tones: **Lyra** with a warm, feminine voice (**Raveena**) and **Mathew** with a confident, masculine tone (**Matthew**). With the ability to interact in both **English** and **Tanglish** (a mix of Tamil and English), CHATGB offers a unique experience that resonates with a global audience.

At its core, the system is powered by **advanced natural language models** that ensure emotionally consistent, fluid conversations. The AI companions are designed to understand and remember past interactions, fostering deeper and more meaningful conversations over time. Users can experience real-time dialogue with Lyra or Mathew, and over time, the system personalizes responses based on prior interactions, adapting to the individual preferences and needs of the user.

## Key Features

### 1. **Two AI Personalities:**
   - **Lyra**: A warm, feminine AI companion with a nurturing and empathetic personality.
   - **Mathew**: A confident, masculine AI with a direct and assertive tone.
   
   These personalities are designed to provide distinct emotional experiences depending on user preference.

### 2. **Real-Time AI Interactions:**
   - The AI companions process user input in real-time, delivering contextually appropriate and emotionally aware responses.
   - The system maintains emotional consistency, creating a deeper connection and engagement with users.

### 3. **Bilingual Communication:**
   - CHATGB supports both **English** and **Tanglish** (Tamil-English mix), making the application accessible to a wide audience, including those fluent in regional languages.

### 4. **Persistent Memory:**
   - The AI companions remember past interactions, which enhances personalization. This allows for more meaningful conversations, with the AI adjusting its responses based on previous chats.

### 5. **Voice Synthesis Integration:**
   - **Amazon Polly** powers high-quality voice synthesis, giving Lyra and Mathew a lifelike and natural-sounding presence.
   - Multiple voice profiles (Raveena for Lyra, Matthew for Mathew) provide a variety of conversational experiences.

### 6. **Dynamic User Interface:**
   - The intuitive chat window adapts seamlessly with dynamic scene transitions and character animations that make conversations visually engaging.

### 7. **Ethical AI & Privacy-Focused:**
   - The system is built with privacy at the forefront. All interactions are securely encrypted, and the user's data is stored with the highest security standards to protect sensitive information.

## Technical Architecture

CHATGB utilizes a combination of **Unity 2022.3 or higher** for its frontend and robust backend systems for AI processing and data management. Here's a breakdown of the technical stack:

- **Frontend:**
  - **Unity** 2022.3 or higher, leveraging **C#** for scripting and animation.
  - Dynamic scene transitions and character animations enhance the user experience.
  
- **Backend:**
  - The backend integrates advanced **AI models** for real-time natural language processing and emotional analysis.
  - **Amazon Polly** provides voice synthesis capabilities, ensuring realistic speech output.
  - A persistent memory system ensures conversations are personalized and fluid over time.

- **Data Management:**
  - Secure data storage and encryption standards are used to ensure user privacy, with sensitive data handled according to industry-leading security protocols.

This integration ensures a lifelike, immersive experience, and the ability to handle multiple audio profiles creates a seamless interaction between the AI companions and the users.
